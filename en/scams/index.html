Recognise a scam

Scammers rush you, play on your emotions and ask for money or bank credentials. Here are the most common scams in Finland and what to do.

1. Stop. Urgency is the scammer's tool.
2. Never give out bank credentials, card details or your personal identity code.
3. Check for yourself from an official source.

If you have already been scammed

020 333Card and online bank blocking service, 24/7 112Emergency number when in danger 116 006Victim Support Finland: help and advice
  1. Call your bank immediately and block your cards and online banking credentials. Your bank's number is on your card and on the bank's website.
  2. Report the crime to the police, also online: poliisi.fi. Keep messages and receipts.
  3. Change passwords the scammer may have seen, starting with your email.
  4. Get support: Victim Support Finland 116 006. Being scammed is nothing to be ashamed of.

Fake fundraiser

A collector at your door or outside a shop asks for money, often for sick children, but cannot give a fundraising permit number.

  • Ask for the fundraising permit number.
  • Check the fundraiser on Keräysvahti and donate only to the account in the permit.
  • Don't open your wallet in front of a stranger.

Fake police officer or bank employee calls

The caller claims your account is being misused and asks for your credentials or to move money to a "safe account".

  • The police or your bank will never ask for your credentials over the phone.
  • Hang up and call your bank's official number yourself.
  • There is no such thing as a "safe account".

Scam messages

Texts or emails about parcels, taxes or your bank, with urgency and a link to a fake website.

  • Don't open the link. Go to the service yourself.
  • Banks never ask you to log in through a link in a message.

"Hi mum, this is my new number"

A message from an unknown number in a family member's name, soon asking you to pay a bill.

  • Call your family member's old number and ask.
  • Don't pay to an unknown account.

Investment and crypto scams

Ads featuring celebrities promise quick profits. An "adviser" gets you to transfer more and more money.

  • Check the company on the Finnish Financial Supervisory Authority's warning list.
  • Never install remote access software at someone's request.

Romance scam

Someone you met online falls in love quickly but can never meet. Then they need money.

  • Never send money to someone you haven't met.
  • Tell someone close to you.
